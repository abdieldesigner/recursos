Most agents shop for real estate media by comparing prices. After 18 years shooting South Florida listings, we think the more useful question is what comes with the price. Do you know what yours includes?

This guide walks through every type of media we shoot in Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach: what each one does for a listing, what it costs, how long it takes, and what is already part of the order before you add a thing.

Why professional media matters in South Florida

Buyers meet the photos before they meet the house. In a market that draws out-of-town and international buyers, the first decision often happens on a screen far from South Florida. Clean, bright photography earns the click; an interactive 3D tour lets a serious buyer walk the home on their own time and arrive at the showing already sure it fits.

The camera is only half of it. The other half is the home itself, which is why we wrote a room-by-room guide to preparing a South Florida listing for photos, 3D and drone. A little prep changes what the camera can do.
